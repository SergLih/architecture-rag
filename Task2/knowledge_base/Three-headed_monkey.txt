The Three Headed Zeta  The Three-Headed Zeta was a mythical zeta that had three heads. It could be found on Zeta Planet. One of Gleb Starborn's often repeated catchphrases is "Look behind you, a three headed hydra!" His usual reason for stating the line would be to momentarily distract someone in order to steal one of their possessions.  Starborn said the line slightly differently while duelling Anya Nebula. "Look behind you, a three headed sea chimp."  The Hidden of Zeta Planet  Sometimes when Gleb is defeated in an Insult Laser Clashing duel, he would also shout, "Look, a three-headed hydra!"  After you free Ivan from Titania Planet Prison without mentioning the capture of Yulia Nova beforehand, he'll consinder of joing Gleb's squad before saying the line and then runs away when he's distracted. When noticing that Ivan is gone he remarked that he couldn't believe he fell for that trick but admited it was a good one.  When caught by the monsters in their village, Starborn may say the line again in order to unsuccessfully make an evac. Ironically a three headed hydra would reveal itself behind them right after, but they won't fall for it a second time.  When Starborn gives the monsters the spirit root which would be used to defeat Darkstar, he is met by the three headed hydra while the monsters are away, preparing the ghost zapping mixture.  When asked by the murderer for something that was distracting but of no real worth to anyone, Starborn could suggest 'A zeta leash with three collars'.  Zeta Planet 2: Darkstar's Revenge  The three headed hydra is mentioned in one of the books in the Vega City Library, where it speculates that the creature was the son of the Loch Ness Monster.  Starborn may also use his line on the Vega Planet Guard and a number of characters.
