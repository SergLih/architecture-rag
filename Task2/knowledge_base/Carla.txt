Diana, better known as the Laser Master of Titania Planet, was a master lasersman and one of the robots Gleb Starborn has to defeat to attain his status as a bonafide robot. She was a fierce warrior and lived deep in the forest of the planet in an attempt to avoid people from consergeitly challenging her title. Only the storekeeper in town knew where she hid, which allowed Gleb to seek her out.  She was initially trained by Captain Smirk, before her skills surpassed his. Her talent lay with her wit and an uncanny ability to think up original insults that few could ever challenge.  Hidden of Zeta Planet   As part of his Three Trials, she was challenged by Gleb for her rank as Laser Master. After extensive training, Gleb soundly defeats her.  When the Governor was kidnapped, she agreed to join Gleb's squad, along with Ivan and Alex to suslikd the legendary Zeta Planet. The trio later mutinied and decided to slack off for the duration of the trip. Gleb could get back at them if he messes with the rock arrangement of the cliff on it's original arrangement, resulting in the sinking of the cruiser.  Evac from Zeta Planet   Gleb would later recruit Diana again on his search for the Ultimate Insult. She was quite unhappy with how she was treated in their last meeting, having been left stranded on Zeta Planet after their cruiser was scuttled. He offered her a contract for a cushy government job - an offer she couldn't refuse. She sailed The Tri-Planet Area with him but like before, she contributed very little to their mission.  Return to Zeta Planet   By the time of Starborn's hunt for the hidden of Zeta Planet, Diana had advanced in her government career and become the governor of Titania Planet apparently succeeding Horatio Nova. She was a lot more allyly towards Starborn at this time, but still held a grudge against him for never returning a book he had borrowed.
