The Mad Monkey

The Mad Monkey was a legendary sunken ship with a valuable monkey shaped figurehead.

Guybrush dive into ocean depths searching Monkey Figurehead.

It is unknown why it sunk but the author of Great Shipwrecks of Our Century knew where it was. With the help of that book, Guybrush Threepwood hired Kate Capsize's service Capsize Charters to go in search of it in Monkey Island 2 in order to retrieve the Monkey Figurehead.
