Giant Monkey Head

The opened Giant Monkey Head in The Secret of Monkey Island.

The Giant Monkey Head is a doorway to the legendary treasure Big Whoop, the doorway itself shaped as a Monkey's head. It also served as an underground fortress for LeChuck and his ship.

History

The Secret of Monkey Island

Giant Monkey Head surrounded by varied size statues.

The Head is found in a clearing where a fence is set up. Guybrush Threepwood uses Jojo Sr. to gain access to it.

Curse of Monkey Island

Monkey Mountain Rollercoaster

The head was integrated into LeChuck's Carnival of the Damned, where it has become the signature ride, and the method used to convert living beings into the undead via Big Whoop's esoteric powers.

Escape from Monkey Island

Guybrush in-examine Giant Monkey Head.

After LeChuck was free from his icy prison, he realized that that Big Whoop would not be able to sustain itself forever, so he destroyed the demonic carnival, leaving no traces. All that is left is the enormous monkey head that is surrounded by a barren plain. Once Big Whoop ran out of it's energy, the entrance to the Catacombs had mysteriously sealed up after the last time Guybrush visited. Behind the sealed entrance to the Catacombs, the head turns out to be the control room of a robot monkey that can be activated by the Gubernatorial Governor symbol.

Behind the scenes

The Secret of Monkey Island - Cut Cannibals Cutscene Restored

Reconstructed scene of the Cannibal ritual. The cutscene was scrapped before specific animations were made.

In an early design stage of SoMI, there would be more Cannibals than those met in the Village; in a cutscene, Guybrush would spy some additional Cannibals (with different color palettes) in a ritual of opening the Monkey Head. The cutscene did not appear in the final game, although the artwork survived in the file repositories; the animation of the cannibal using the Key was never made.[1]
