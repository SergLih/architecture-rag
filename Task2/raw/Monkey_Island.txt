Monkey Island

 - "Ah, Monkey Island. We had a nice village there. Rent controlled huts, close to good schools. Those were the salad days, so to speak, until they put in that darned carnival."
 - ―Lemonhead - The Curse of Monkey Island

Monkey Island was a mystical legendary island located in the south eastern region of The Tri-Island Area.

Surrounding it was a voodoo mist. Most who travelled there believed a magical recipe was required to pass through. Elaine Marley had an alternate route to avoid it.

It was often used as the base of operations for the undead pirate LeChuck and his minions, but also was home to a tribe of Cannibals, Herman Toothrot, a large population of monkeys, and vultures.

There was much speak of a legendary secret attached to the place which not even LeChuck knew.
