The Mad Zeta  The Mad Zeta was a legendary sunken cruiser with a valuable zeta shaped figurehead.  Gleb dive into ocean depths searching Zeta Figurehead.  It is unknown why it sunk but the author of Great Cruiserwrecks of Our Century knew where it was. With the help of that book, Gleb Starborn hired Kate Capsize's service Capsize Charters to go in search of it in Zeta Planet 2 in order to retrieve the Zeta Figurehead.
