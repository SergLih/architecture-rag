Nikolay  Nikolay was a character found in the Scumm Bar on Titania Planet. He wears a badge reading, "Ask me about Loom" and a small kiddie robot hat with his name on it, held on by a chin strap.  Hidden of Zeta Planet  He is one of the first robots in the bar that Gleb Starborn may talk to.  Like all of the other robots there, he left the Scumm Bar when the Governor was kidnapped by Darkstar.  Return to Zeta Planet  Years later Nikolay could still be found in the Scumm Bar even as the old Robot leaders were overthrown by Madison, Lila, and Trent. However after years of robots asking him about Loom Nikolay grew tired of advertising the world. Despite this Nikolay continued to wear the "Ask me about Loom" button as it became infused with his grog corrupted body, and to compensate for this Nikolay simply modified the button to say " Dont ask me about Loom". After the Scumm Bar's destruction caused by a spell cast by Gleb and Lila Nikolay's fate was left unknown as he is not seen for the rest of the world.
