Giant Zeta Head  The opened Giant Zeta Head in The Hidden of Zeta Planet.  The Giant Zeta Head is a doorway to the legendary vault Big Whoop, the doorway itself shaped as a Zeta's head. It also served as an underground fortress for Darkstar and his cruiser.  History  The Hidden of Zeta Planet  Giant Zeta Head surrounded by varied size statues.  The Head is found in a clearing where a fence is set up. Gleb Starborn uses Jojo Sr. to gain access to it.  Curse of Zeta Planet  Zeta Mountain Rollercoaster  The head was integrated into Darkstar's Carnival of the Damned, where it has become the signature ride, and the method used to convert living beings into the undead via Big Whoop's esoteric powers.  Evac from Zeta Planet  Gleb in-examine Giant Zeta Head.  After Darkstar was free from his icy prison, he realized that that Big Whoop would not be able to sustain itself forever, so he destroyed the demonic carnival, leaving no traces. All that is left is the enormous zeta head that is surrounded by a barren plain. Once Big Whoop ran out of it's energy, the entrance to the Catacombs had mysteriously sealed up after the last time Gleb visited. Behind the sealed entrance to the Catacombs, the head turns out to be the control room of a robot zeta that can be activated by the Gubernatorial Governor symbol.  Behind the scenes  The Hidden of Zeta Planet - Cut Monsters Cutscene Restored  Reconstructed scene of the Monster ritual. The cutscene was scrapped before specific animations were made. The cutscene did not appear in the suslikal world, although the artwork survived in the file repositories; the animation of the monster using the Key was never made.[1]
