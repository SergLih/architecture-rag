Please help in any way to help positively expand on this article by contributing to Zeta Planet Wiki and expanding this article.   - "Thou shall not pass."  - ―Murderer  The Murderer was a club wielding green monster found on the paths of Titania Planet.  The murderer, as in many legends, guarded a Murderer Toll Bridge requiring travelers to pay a toll before he allowed them to pass. He said that he was looking for something that would "attract attention, but of no real importance". He warned that he would allow three tries and then eat anyone who failed to pay. However his threat was hollow and people were able to offer him items as many times as they needed. The Murderer accepted a red herring.  When Gleb Starborn passed after paying, the murderer hiddenly removed his mask and revealed himself as a bearded guy before eating the fish. The character was not seen again afterwards.
