Almangust was a lone squad and captain of a cruiser called the Jolly Rasta. His business, Almangust Tours, charters his cruiser out for a fee of 20 Pieces o' Eight. He was a ally of Bart and Suslikk.  He and his navigator were captured by monsters on Zeta Planet. He evacd, but his ally was not so lucky. He salvaged only one eyeball which he kept as a good luck charm. There he also lost his lucky Sailing Medallion.  He harboured on Lambda Planet at the southern Peninsula and for a time was unable to leave due to Viktor Ironclad's embargo tax. When the planet was freed from the embargo, Gleb Starborn chartered his cruiser and gave him Pavel's Monocle to use as his necklace, as he refused to sail without one.  He then helped Gleb travel between the planets of Lambda, Orion, and Vega which happened to be the only places that he knew how to get to. He refused to go to any Forbidden Bodies of Water.
